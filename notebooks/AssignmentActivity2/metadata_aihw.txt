IMPORTANT PROVENANCE NOTE
This is a SYNTHETIC teaching dataset created for the Monash Data Analytics
Career Accelerator. It is modelled on the structure and published national
patterns of the Australian Institute of Health and Welfare (AIHW) MyHospitals
emergency department (ED) care collection (National Non-Admitted Patient
Emergency Department Care Database), 2023-24 reference year. All hospital
names are fictional. Values are statistically plausible but do not describe
any real hospital, patient, or event. Do not cite this data outside the
program. Real AIHW ED data: https://www.aihw.gov.au/hospitals

SCENARIO
You have been contracted by the AIHW to analyse emergency department
performance across a representative extract of 60 Australian public hospital
EDs, covering 1 July 2023 to 30 June 2025 (financial years 2023-24 and
2024-25). The files below were assembled from different reporting systems;
treat identifier and format consistency as something to verify, not assume.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FILE 1: ed_presentations.csv
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
One row per hospital, per month, per triage category.
Monthly counts of ED presentations and subsequent admissions.

  hospital_id       Hospital identifier (join key to hospital_metadata.xlsx)
  month             First day of the reporting month, YYYY-MM-DD
  triage_code       Australasian Triage Scale (ATS) category, integer 1-5
  triage_category   Triage code with label, e.g. "3 Urgent"
  presentations     Count of ED presentations in the month
  admissions        Count of those presentations subsequently admitted
                    to hospital (blank where the feed did not report)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FILE 2: waiting_times_regional.csv
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
One row per hospital, per month, per triage category.
Timeliness measures extracted from a separate regional reporting system.

  hospital_id                     Hospital identifier (verify formatting
                                  before joining across files)
  month                           Reporting month, MMM-YYYY (e.g. Jul-2023)
  triage_code                     ATS category, integer 1-5
  median_wait_to_treatment_mins   Median minutes from presentation to start
                                  of clinical care
  pct_seen_on_time                Percentage of patients seen within the ATS
                                  clinically recommended time for their
                                  triage category (blank = not reported)
  median_ed_stay_mins             Median total minutes spent in the ED

ATS clinically recommended maximum waiting times:
  1 Resuscitation  immediate      4 Semi-urgent   60 minutes
  2 Emergency      10 minutes     5 Non-urgent   120 minutes
  3 Urgent         30 minutes

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FILE 3: hospital_metadata.xlsx
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Sheet "hospitals" — one row per hospital (62 rows; not all hospitals
reported activity during the extract window):
  hospital_id, hospital_name, state, peer_group, remoteness_area, ed_beds

  peer_group values (AIHW hospital peer group classification):
    Principal referral | Public acute group A | Public acute group B |
    Public acute group C | Women's and children's
  remoteness_area values (ASGS): Major Cities | Inner Regional |
    Outer Regional | Remote | Very Remote

Sheet "state_populations" — one row per state/territory:
  state, population_2024 (Australian Bureau of Statistics estimated
  resident population, used for attendance-rate-per-1,000 calculations)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FILE 4: health_tweets.csv
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
480 synthetic social media posts (1 July 2025 to 30 June 2026) about Australian
emergency department experiences, collected for a sentiment and topic analysis
pilot. All hospitals are unnamed and all events fictional; any resemblance to
real posts is coincidental. The file contains no personal or sensitive
information under the Privacy Act 1988, so it is safe to send to an external
AI API (Module 5).

  tweet_id      integer, unique record identifier (1001-1480)
  created_at    ISO date, YYYY-MM-DD
  state         Australian state or territory of the poster (weighted to
                population)
  tweet_text    the post text

Deliberate content mix (for the Topic 5.3 evaluation work): plain negative,
neutral and positive posts (approximately 41 / 31 / 28 per cent); sarcastic
negatives with positive surface wording; mixed-sentiment posts (the dominant
clause carries the label); negation cases ("not unhappy"); clinical-vocabulary
neutral posts.

Note: this file has no sentiment or topic labels. Creating a manually
labelled ground-truth subset is part of the Module 5 work (5.3.5). The
Module 5 files include health_tweets_groundtruth_template.csv (50 sampled
rows from this file, with empty label and comments columns) and one
acceptable labelling of the same 50 rows.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
RELATIONSHIPS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ed_presentations.csv  --- hospital_id --->  hospital_metadata.xlsx (hospitals)
waiting_times_regional.csv --- hospital_id ---> hospital_metadata.xlsx
ed_presentations.csv  <-- hospital_id + month + triage_code -->
                                              waiting_times_regional.csv
hospital_metadata.xlsx (hospitals.state) ---> state_populations.state
health_tweets.csv     --- no key; relates by time period and theme only

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PLANNING BENCHMARK (used in Assignment activity 6)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
benchmark_capacity_monthly = 165000

The agreed planning capacity across the 60 reporting hospitals, expressed as
ED presentations per month. Months in which total presentations exceed this
figure are treated as operating above planned capacity. Use it as the
horizontal reference line in the utilisation chart (Assignment activity 6,
Question 1).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BUSINESS QUESTIONS (from the AIHW brief)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 1. How do ED presentations vary over time - by month, season, triage
    category, hospital peer group, and state/territory?
 2. Where and when are patients not being seen within clinically
    recommended times, and is timeliness improving or deteriorating?
 3. How do attendance rates per 1,000 population compare across states?
 4. What staffing and resourcing recommendations does the evidence support?
 5. What does public social media sentiment suggest about community
    experience of EDs, and how reliable is that signal?
